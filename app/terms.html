What Leanward is

Leanward is a personal tool for tracking food, weight and measurements, and for following a goal phase such as a cut, recomp, lean bulk or maintenance. It is an early version shared with a small group, free of charge, and it may change or have bugs.

Not medical advice

Leanward gives estimates and general information. It is not medical, nutritional or fitness advice, and it doesn't diagnose or treat anything. Calorie and nutrient targets come from published formulas and reference values, shown with their working; your real needs can differ. Food data comes from public databases and from what people enter, and can be wrong: check it against the label.

Talk to a doctor or registered dietitian before changing how you eat if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, have a medical condition (for example diabetes, kidney disease or an eating disorder, now or in the past), or take medication affected by diet. If tracking starts to feel stressful or takes over, stop, and see the Support section in the app.

Who can use it

Leanward is for adults 18 and over. You sign in with your own Google account and are responsible for what you enter.

Your data

What you save is yours. You can export it or delete your account and everything in it at any time from Profile. How data is stored and used is described in Privacy.

Leanward is provided as it is, without warranties of any kind. It may be unavailable at times, and although it is built to keep your data safe and backed up, that can't be guaranteed: export anything you can't afford to lose. To the extent the law allows, the owner isn't liable for losses that come from using it or relying on its numbers.
